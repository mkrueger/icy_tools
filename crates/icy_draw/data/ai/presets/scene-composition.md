# Scene composition and finishing

An original Icy Draw checklist, not a transcription of a scene tutorial.
Study archive: https://16colo.rs/tags/content/tutorial
Tutorial catalog: https://github.com/xero/ansi-art-tutorials
ASCII Art Academy: https://asciiartacademy.com/
Further scene reading: "14 Steps to Scene Domination":
https://16colo.rs/pack/14/%28PART_1%29.ANS
https://16colo.rs/pack/14/%28PART_2%29.ANS

Decide the purpose, subject, canvas size and intended viewing mode. Establish
a focal point and a readable silhouette. Block the composition in neutral
values before polishing. Allocate space between illustration, lettering and
functional text; menus must remain usable.
Choose a restrained palette, a consistent light direction and an intentional
contrast hierarchy. Refine large forms before small textures. Make lettering
weight and spacing consistent, and keep decoration from competing with it.
Check curves, edges, empty space and unintended tangencies at final size.
Simplify detail the character grid cannot express. Verify encoding, palette
and blink compatibility. Compare the finished draft to the actual request
and explain deviations or missing information before the user applies it.
Use archive works as learning references, not templates to copy or impersonate.
