# Outline-first and palette experimentation

Original workflow incorporating the user's supplied community advice:
begin with light-gray outlines / large "blobs", then experiment with color
replacement. Community: https://www.reddit.com/r/ANSIart/
No specific Reddit thread was independently verified; do not attribute these
steps to a particular author or quote them as a retrieved tutorial.

Read the current canvas and choose a neutral light gray from its actual
palette. Sketch the subject as large masses, leaving useful negative space.
Check placement, silhouette and proportions before adding color.
Assign a small set of material colors and separate structure from accents.
Try one coherent palette change at a time, preserving cell characters and
foreground/background roles. Limit replacements to the intended subject
or region so text labels and unrelated art retain their colors.
The assistant has no Replace Color tool: with tools, read the bounded target
region and update matching cells in batches using icy_set_cells. Do not claim
to invoke a tool that does not exist. In advice-only mode, suggest the editor's
manual color-replacement workflow instead.
Review the result at normal size and keep the experiment in the preview draft.
