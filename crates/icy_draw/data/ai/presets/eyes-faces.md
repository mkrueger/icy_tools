# Eyes and face construction

Original exercise informed by general low-resolution drawing techniques.
Further study (no tutorial text or artwork is bundled):
- Enzo's eye construction: https://www.roysac.com/tutorial/blockasciitut-z0-eye.html
- ZeroVision's Chucky walkthrough: https://www.roysac.com/tutorial/ansitut-zv.html
- Archived ZeroVision tutorial: https://16colo.rs/pack/blocktronics_resvolution/zv-tutorial.ans
- ASCII Art Academy: https://asciiartacademy.com/
- The Knight/Fuel's face revision guide (accessible mirror):
  https://www.roysac.com/tutorial/ansitut-tk-fluph.html

Choose an original subject rather than reproducing a tutorial character.
Set the eye line, head envelope and expression at the actual cell aspect ratio.
Start with a limited-value sketch; establish both eyes at a consistent scale
before expanding to the nose, mouth and jaw. Inspect negative space between
features and correct proportions before shading.
Use deliberate segment lengths for curves; avoid alternating lengths without
a shape reason. Keep pupils and highlights readable at final viewing size.
If symmetry is useful, use it as a starting scaffold, then vary brows, eyelids
and cheek contours to communicate expression. Do not mechanically mirror
every detail. Preserve feature separation and simplify detail that exceeds
the resolution. Use negative space to separate hair from the face and choose
hair highlights consistent with the light direction; avoid abrupt unrelated
highlight colors unless the entire design intentionally uses that style.
In text-only ASCII, choose printable characters by visual
weight; in CP437 use available blocks/half-blocks. Inspect the whole silhouette
before applying local highlights. Batch edits and finish with a bounds check.
